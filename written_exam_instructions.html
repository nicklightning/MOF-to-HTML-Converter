General Information:

The exam timetable will be made available here: Exam Dates and Timetable and you will also receive an e-mail from the exams office when it is published. Information about exams is available from the university exams office: Exam Information. You should ensure you are familiar with all the exam rules: Exam Rules including those which apply to calculators and electronic devices.

Adjustments to standard exam arrangements are made in response to specific learning difficulties and disabilities (Link to Inclusive Arrangements). Approach Student Wellbeing Services to discuss a Student Support Plan if you require exam adjustments. 

Examples of past exam papers (up to 3 years old) are available from the university library's past exam paper repository: Past Exam Papers. You should consult your teachers where you have specific questions about the usefulness of past papers.

 

Feedback:

Feedback on your attempt will be available after the exam. The e-mail you receive to announce the release of your results will contain instructions on how you can request feedback. You should also check the information provided in the SNES Student Bulletin shortly before the exam period for January (for Semester 1 exams), May (for Semester 2 exams) or August (for resit/deferral exams)).
