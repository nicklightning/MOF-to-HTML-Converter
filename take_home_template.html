General Information:

The exam timetable, including 24h take home exams will be made available here: Exam Dates and Timetable (e.g. it will state ‘24h Take Home Exam’ in brackets after the module code) and you will also receive an e-mail from the exams office when it is published.

You are not expected to, and it is not advisable to, work on an assessment constantly for the 24-hour period. The assessment will have been designed to be worked on for a much shorter period of time in line with the length of a standard exam sitting.

When answering the exam you should observe normal exam conditions and must not communicate/collude with other students taking the paper. The work that you submit must be your own work. Your submission may be submitted to the Turnitin similarity checker as a check on plagiarism.

If you are unfamiliar with submitting your work there is Canvas guidance available for submitting a Canvas Assignment.

There is further student guidance on assignments available on the Digital Learning web pages and in the Canvas Student Orientation Course

 

General Instructions for 24 hr Take-Home Exams:

The following instructions will be shown on the frontsheet of the exam. The frontsheet will also report how many questions you should answer (if the paper gives students a choice of questions), the maximum word count permitted and any specific external resources you are required to use (if appropriate).

  • A submission point has been created under Assignments (click on "Assignments" in the left panel) of your Canvas Course. Your submission should be a single file uploaded through this submission point. To avoid system overload please submit in good time within the 24 hour period.
  • For the specific exam you are taking, contact information for academic queries during the first hour of the 24 hour window will be provided.
  • Contact information for technical queries: University IT support is available 24/7. If you have any issues accessing your assessment or University IT systems you can contact IT support on +44 (0)191 208 5999 at any time, or by email at it.servicedesk@ncl.ac.uk (monitored 08:00-17:00 BST, Monday to Friday).
  • A submission of up to 30 minutes late is permitted without penalty or the requirement to submit a PEC (because of the challenges presented by remote assessment/IT difficulties). Submissions received from 30 minutes onwards will receive a mark of zero.
  • You can request to defer an exam to the next normal occasion (usually August) by submitting a PEC form. Extensions of 24 hr exams cannot be approved as a PEC concession.

 

Feedback:

Feedback on your attempt will be available after the exam. The e-mail you receive to announce the release of your results will contain instructions on how you can request feedback. You should also check the information provided in the SNES Student Bulletin shortly before the exam period for January (for Semester 1 exams), May (for Semester 2 exams) or August (for resit/deferral exams)).
